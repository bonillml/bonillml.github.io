Is this tweet useful in a disaster?

By Stella Joung, Mandolyn Loveland, and Matt Bonilla

Add a tweet as its text and photos. A model reads the text and the images together and reports whether the post carries information a response team could use.

Informative
Useful for humanitarian aid, such as reports of damage, needs, or people affected.
Not informative
Nothing a response team could act on.

Paste a link from x.com or twitter.com. The server fetches the text and photos for you.

    What the model read

    The model is a CLIP-based classifier that fuses image and text, trained on the CrisisMMD dataset to separate informative from not informative tweets. Predictions can be wrong, so check anything urgent by hand.
